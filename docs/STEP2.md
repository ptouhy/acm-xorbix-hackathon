## What to notice

| Query | Business insight |
|-------|------------------|
| Patient status | How many Active vs Lapsed vs Churned |
| Lead funnel | Conversion rate by stage |
| Lead sources | Referral vs ads vs walk-in |
| Visit revenue | Top services by dollars |
| No-shows | Lost capacity / revenue |
| Marketing ROI | Cheapest channel per conversion |
| High churn risk | Retention target list size |
